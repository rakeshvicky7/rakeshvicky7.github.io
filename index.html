About me

Mechanical engineer turned computer scientist

I hold a Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering and am completing an M.Sc. in Computer Science at IU International University of Applied Sciences in Berlin. That first degree still shapes how I approach software: establish what a system is actually doing before deciding what to change about it.

My focus is data, machine learning and artificial intelligence, with a growing interest in quantum computing. I work through a subject until I understand the mechanism rather than only the interface, which is also why I am comfortable close to the machine — operating systems, networks, and the point where hardware and software meet.

Before moving to Germany I spent a year in client-facing roles in India, advising prospective students on programmes and study-abroad decisions. It taught me to establish what someone actually needs, explain a complex option in plain terms, and follow through — which is how I try to write about technical work as well.
